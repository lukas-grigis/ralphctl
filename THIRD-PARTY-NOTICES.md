# Third-party notices

ralphctl bundles skill content adapted from the following MIT-licensed upstream projects. The
adapted copies live under `src/integration/ai/skills/bundled/` (shipped as `dist/skills/`);
verbatim upstream snapshots used for drift review live under `scripts/vendor/skills/`. Each
upstream's license and copyright notice is reproduced in full below, as the MIT license requires.

- `ralphctl-debugging-and-error-recovery`, `ralphctl-code-review-and-quality`,
  `ralphctl-test-driven-development`, `ralphctl-idea-refinement`, `ralphctl-security-and-hardening`,
  `ralphctl-spec-driven-development`, `ralphctl-api-and-interface-design`,
  `ralphctl-performance-optimization`, `ralphctl-frontend-ui-engineering`,
  `ralphctl-deprecation-and-migration`, `ralphctl-code-simplification`,
  `ralphctl-source-driven-development`, `ralphctl-observability-and-instrumentation`, and the
  risk-first slicing in `ralphctl-iterative-review` — from
  [addyosmani/agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills)
- `ralphctl-ponytail`, and the concrete-case and expected-load review rules in
  `ralphctl-code-review-and-quality` — from [DietrichGebert/ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail)
- `ralphctl-domain-driven-design` — from [wondelai/skills](https://github.com/wondelai/skills)
- `ralphctl-codebase-design`, `ralphctl-writing-for-agents`, and parts of
  `ralphctl-debugging-and-error-recovery`, `ralphctl-test-driven-development`, and
  `ralphctl-code-review-and-quality` — from [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)
- `ralphctl-humanizer` — concept from [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer); its pattern
  catalogue is written fresh in ralphctl's own words
