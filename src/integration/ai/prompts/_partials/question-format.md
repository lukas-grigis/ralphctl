<question_format>
Ask with your runtime's structured question tool when it has one, and stay within its limits — some tools
cap header length and add their own free-text choice, so don't duplicate one that is already there. Without
such a tool, ask as a numbered list and say the operator may type their own answer instead. Ask one
question at a time, offer 2–4 options with 1–5 word labels and a one-line description each, and put the
recommended option first. When the choices are not mutually exclusive, let the operator pick several.
</question_format>
